# Square Packing

Version 0.2.0 · [Play it](https://tangentmeasure.com/square-packing-game/) · MIT License

A browser game on packing 4 to 8 squares, sides parallel to the frame, into the smallest
square frame. The frame side is L\*ₙ, computed exactly from the closed formulas for n ≤ 8;
every answer and every hint comes from those formulas and their canonical packings, with
no search.

The formulas are proved in two papers:
[arXiv:2610.22622](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22622) and
[arXiv:2610.28504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28504).

The whole game is one file, `docs/index.html`, which GitHub Pages serves as it is. It needs
no server and makes no network requests: the fonts are inlined, and progress and settings
are kept in the browser's local storage.

Languages: English, plus drafts in Spanish (Latin American), French, Brazilian Portuguese,
Hindi and Simplified Chinese. The game starts in the browser's language and remembers a
choice made from the globe menu at the top; a draft is labelled as such in its own language
until a native speaker has reviewed it. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to review or
add a language.

## Playing

- **Levels** (the default) are 29 fixed puzzles, one for each strategy that can decide L\*ₙ
  up to eight squares: windowpane, shelf and column for 4 to 6 squares, the three 7-square
  strategies, and the 23 at 8 squares. Stars give the difficulty, from one to four. Every
  level is open from the start; a level whose answer extends an earlier one links back to
  it.
- Under the board: **Hint** gives up to four steps (how much the smallest square costs,
  the corner squares, the lines of squares that span the frame, one square in place);
  **Show me how** draws the standard packing; **Start over** sends every square back to the
  tray; **Skip** moves on. When the frame is full, the solved card gives the time and the
  plain reason the frame cannot be smaller, with those lines of squares ringed on the board.
- **Free play** serves random puzzles of 4 to 8 squares (every strategy at that size in
  turn) or plays side lengths you type. With **Advanced** on, the answer also names the
  strategy and shows its formula and the full strategy table.
- The **menu** lists the levels (a solved level shows its packing), moves the packing
  (push, turn, mirror, shuffle), and holds the display settings: colours (automatic, dark,
  light) and the labels on the squares.
- Keyboard: on the board, `1`–`8` pick a square, `Enter` places it, arrows move it to the
  next edge (`Alt` for one unit), `Delete` sends it back, `Shift` + arrows push everything,
  `Q` `E` `H` `V` turn and mirror the packing.
- A puzzle can be shared as a link: the side lengths follow `#`, as in
  `…/square-packing-game/#19.16.13.12.10.9.8`. A link opens in Free play.

## Source

    src/formula.js   values of L*_n, n <= 8, strategy ledger, canonical placements
    src/board.js     board logic (snapping, forgiving drops, gravity, win check, keyboard
                     moves) and canvas drawing
    src/reveal.js    answer panel text, the plain "why" sentence, shadow bars, hint ladder
    src/levels.js    the 29 levels: order, stars, puzzles (order from tools/level_order.py)
    src/progress.js  saved progress and settings (browser storage), carry-over from v0.1.0
    src/serve.js     which random puzzle comes next in Free play
    src/i18n.js      the language layer and the English strings (the reference)
    src/lang/*.js    one file per other language
    src/bank.js      the Free play seed bank (generated by tools/build_bank.js)
    src/fonts/       font subsets and their licenses (copied by tools/fonts.js)
    src/page.html    page template (open docs/index.html to play, not this)
    tools/           the generators above, the level-order script, the colour-blindness
                     check of the tile palette, and the translation review workbooks

    node build.js       # writes docs/index.html
    npm test            # seven suites, about 30 seconds
    npm run test:full   # larger sweeps of every suite (several minutes), plus
                        # test/formula.test.js, which needs test/fixture_L8.json, made by:
                        # python3 test/make_fixture.py path/to/verify_L8_general.py

The version number lives in `package.json`; the build writes it into the page.

# Empaquetado de cuadrados

Un juego de navegador: hay que acomodar de 4 a 8 cuadrados, con los lados paralelos al
marco, en el marco cuadrado más pequeño posible. El lado del marco es L\*ₙ, calculado de
forma exacta con las fórmulas cerradas para n ≤ 8; todas las soluciones y pistas salen de
esas fórmulas y de sus empaquetados canónicos, sin búsqueda. Las fórmulas se demuestran
en [arXiv:2610.22622](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22622) y
[arXiv:2610.28504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28504).

El juego tiene 29 niveles, uno por cada estrategia hasta ocho cuadrados, y un modo de juego
libre con desafíos al azar o con los lados que tú escribas. Todo el juego es un solo
archivo, `docs/index.html`, que funciona sin servidor y no hace peticiones a otros sitios.
Empieza en el idioma del navegador y recuerda la elección hecha en el menú de idiomas. La
traducción al español (latinoamericano) es un borrador pendiente de revisión.
